About Us

A little more of why we do what we do...

AI Tactical Marketing was created by a veteran who spent two decades building businesses, solving problems, and helping people grow. After years of watching small businesses struggle with marketing — high costs, confusing tools, inconsistent results — we set out to build something better.

This platform blends real-world marketing experience with the accuracy and discipline learned in the military. The mission: give every business owner a tactical advantage through simple, fast, and effective AI-powered marketing. We believe in authenticity, precision, and service. We believe small businesses deserve access to powerful tools without outrageous prices. And we believe marketing should feel empowering — not overwhelming.

This is marketing built differently.

This is marketing done right.
